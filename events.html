Meet Your Event Planner

Rachel Kret

Rachel has a passion for bringing people together and creating events that leave a lasting impression. With nearly a decade of experience in hospitality, she has worked everywhere from fine dining restaurants to neighborhood dive bars, giving her a well-rounded perspective on exceptional guest experiences. Over the past five years, Rachel has planned and coordinated large-scale events, corporate gatherings, weddings, and private celebrations. She enjoys working closely with each client to create a planning experience that feels seamless, personal, and stress-free, with the goal of creating celebrations that guests will remember long after the event is over. Rachel is pursuing her bachelor's degree in Public Relations at Wayne State University, where she continues to strengthen her expertise in communication, marketing, and relationship building.
