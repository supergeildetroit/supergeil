Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting, dining, celebrating, or planning an event at Supergeil.

Reservations & Dining

Do you take reservations?

Yes. Make a reservation through OpenTable. Walk-ins are also welcome when space is available.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome, but reservations are preferred and prioritized. You can reserve a table through OpenTable.

Do you offer vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free options?

We can accommodate some dietary needs, but we cannot accommodate Kosher or Halal requirements. Cross-contamination is always a risk, so please speak with your server about any allergies or dietary restrictions before ordering.

Do you offer brunch?

Yes. Brunch is served on Sundays from 11am–3pm. View our current brunch menu online.

Can I view the current menus online?

Yes. Visit our Menus page to view brunch, lunch, dinner, dessert, and drink menus.

Do you have a kids menu?

No. We do not currently offer a separate kids menu.

Can you accommodate large parties?

We can seat parties of up to 15 guests together. Larger groups should inquire through our Private Events page.

Can our group request separate checks?

No. We do not offer separate checks. An automatic gratuity is added for parties of six or more.

Can we bring outside food, drinks, or a celebration cake?

Outside food and beverages are not permitted. Celebration cakes may be brought in with a outside dessert fee of $5 per guest. Outside wine is subject to a $25 corkage fee.

Do you serve Two James spirits?

Yes. We proudly serve Two James spirits. Please check the current drink menu for more information.

Visiting Supergeil

Where is Supergeil located?

We are located at 2442 Michigan Avenue in Detroit’s Corktown neighborhood.

Where can I park?

Street parking and nearby public parking options are available throughout Corktown. Please allow a little extra time during busy evenings and neighborhood events.

Is Supergeil wheelchair accessible?

Please contact the restaurant before your visit so our team can confirm current accessibility details and help make your arrival as smooth as possible.

Are children welcome?

Families are welcome. We recommend contacting the restaurant ahead of time if you need a high chair or have other seating needs.

Is patio seating available?

Yes. Patio seating is available on a first-come, first-served basis and is weather permitting.

Are pets allowed?

Pets are welcome on the patio.

Private Events

Can I host a private event at Supergeil?

Yes. We host weddings, corporate gatherings, birthdays, graduations, retirement parties, and other private celebrations. Visit our Private Events page to learn more.

How do I request information about hosting an event?

Use our Private Events inquiry form. Our event planner will follow up about your preferred date, party size, and event needs.

Can I view private-event pricing?

Yes. View our current Private Events pricing guide, or visit the Private Events page for additional details.

Is there a nearby place for out-of-town guests to stay?

Alles Paletti is a two-bedroom, two-bathroom loft located above Supergeil. View Alles Paletti on Airbnb, or learn more on our Private Events page.

Ordering & Contact

Can I order food online?

Yes. Order online through Toast.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards can be purchased online through Toast.
